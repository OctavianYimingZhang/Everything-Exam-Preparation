# Notes Protocol

## Purpose

Notes reconstruct the teachable course for revision. They support a student who may be learning the material for the first time and remain broader than a past-paper trend report.

## Coverage

Build knowledge units from connected definitions, mechanisms, methods, comparisons, calculations, data interpretation, evidence, applications, and explanatory examples. Cover every substantive instructional source. Combine repetition and continuation slides into the nearest complete explanation.

Use learning outcomes, agendas, section dividers, recaps, and summaries to organise the document. Use substantive slides for detailed explanation. Use administrative, decorative, citation-only, empty, and duplicated material as internal audit context.

Past papers and recap questions calibrate explanation depth and highlight assessable relationships. The public Notes remain organised by course logic.

## Slide Triage

The source processor assigns each slide-like fragment:

- `use`: contributes structure or substantive teaching;
- `merge_with_previous`: continues or duplicates the preceding knowledge unit;
- `exclude`: contributes no teaching content to the Notes.

It also records `notes_role`, `detailed_explanation_allowed`, and `triage_reason`. Review the source-level audit before finalising coverage.

## Writing

Explain what each concept means, why it matters, how it works, how it is measured or tested, and how to interpret or apply it. Define specialist terms at first use. Show causal chains explicitly. Place comparisons, equations, methods, and worked examples inside the knowledge unit they support.

Use visible equations and define symbols. Carry units through calculations. Explain assumptions and the biological or physical meaning of the result.

## Visuals

Choose diagrams, graphs, micrographs, tables, and experimental schematics that materially improve understanding. Crop or extract the relevant region at readable resolution. Add a concise explanatory caption with the source filename and slide or page locator.

## Document Design

Use A4, Arial, 2.5 cm margins, 1.5 line spacing, a centred main title, left-aligned headings, justified body text, black academic text, restrained hierarchy, compact tables, centred display equations, and consistent figure placement.

Use the user's requested filename and language. Otherwise use English and a clear course-based filename. Use a single main title by default and add supplementary title text when requested.

## Verification

Confirm every instructional source appears in the internal coverage audit. Check heading order, equation visibility, table width, image resolution, captions, and page breaks. Render the complete document and inspect every page.
