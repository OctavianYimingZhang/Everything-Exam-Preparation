# Exam Preparation Notes protocol

Default artifact: `Exam_Preparation_Notes.docx`.

## Notes structure

A useful notes output normally contains:

1. Course knowledge map: what the course teaches and how topics connect.
2. Exam habit analysis: question types, command words, repeated topics, mark patterns, and examiner preferences.
3. Exam preparation notes: topic-by-topic explanations written for revision.
4. Mode-specific preparation when MCQ, short answer, long answer, or essay material is present.

## Writing approach

Use headings, compact tables, worked examples, short lists, and paragraphs whenever they help the student revise faster. Expand content that is repeated in practice material or difficult to apply. Compress peripheral material.

## Practical and data material

When practical, data, or problem material appears, include method steps, controls, readouts, graph/table interpretation, calculations, limitations, and example answer moves.
