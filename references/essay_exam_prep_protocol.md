# Essay exam prep protocol

Essay preparation should turn course material into paragraphs and arguments that can be used under exam conditions.

## Output pieces

- Module-covering essay questions.
- Thesis options.
- Argument maps.
- Exam-ready paragraphs.
- Example essays when requested.

## Paragraph style

Each paragraph should make a clear claim, explain it in course language, connect it to the likely essay question, and show why it matters for the argument.
